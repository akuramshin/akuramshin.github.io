  As a Research Engineer Intern at the [Acceleration Consortium](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/sdls/sdl0-ai-and-automation), I contributed to chemistry lab automation efforts by leading the end-to-end development of a [UMI](https://umi-gripper.github.io/)-style handheld data collection device. My work spanned both hardware and software, from mechanism and sensor design to building the full capture and dataset generation pipeline. I developed robust systems for pose and gripper estimation, synchronized sensors under camera latency, and implemented quality filtering and normalization for VLA policy training. By making systematic decisions regarding data quality, observation inputs, and action representations, I successfully improved VLA policy (fine-tuned on UMI data) success rates by 20% during real-robot deployment.
